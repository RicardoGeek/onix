How it works. Every signal is a real 7Wave star or arrow, on gold H1 or M15, 2018-2026, picked at random. You see the chart only up to the bar where the signal appeared. The date and the price are hidden, so memory can't help. Decide the way you would live, from what is on the screen.

After you decide the future is shown. The shaded area is what came next. The dashed lines are the entry, the -1R stop (1.5 ATR) and the +3R target. You also see how far the move ran and what the whale exit (holding) would have made. Your answer can't be changed after the reveal.

Saving. Decisions are saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back any time. 100 decisions give a first read, and 300 or more give a reliable one. When you're done, download the results and send the file to Claude.
